# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Serious football fans — the tactics nerds. People who already watch the matches, follow a club closely, and have run out of road with scores and punditry. They arrive mid-season, often mid-match, usually on a phone or a second screen, and they want depth: why a side is winning, whether a rumour is real, what the model actually thinks and how sure it is. They are fluent enough to spot a lazy number, so the interface has to survive scrutiny rather than impress at a glance.

The growth surface (Intelligence library, share cards, search) speaks to the same person before they have an account. It is an entry point, not a separate audience.

## Product Purpose

FootIQ is an AI football operating system: live match intelligence, scouting, tactics, predictions, fantasy and history in one place, reasoning about the game rather than just reporting it. Success right now is retention — beta users who come back weekly without being prompted. The habit is the proof; traffic and revenue follow it.

## Positioning

An AI football OS, not a scores site. Every screen should be doing something a scoreboard cannot: inferring, comparing, explaining, or predicting — and saying how confident it is.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, honest, floodlit. The voice of a good analyst: confident because the work is shown, never because it is loud. It states what it measured, states what it guessed, and never dresses one as the other. Broadcast-night atmosphere in the surfaces, analyst restraint in the language. No hype, no exclamation, no selling.

Under the hood it wants to feel like **Linear or Raycast** — keyboard-first, dense, chrome that gets out of the way. Speed and precision are the aesthetic; the football is the drama.

## Anti-references

- **Betting sites** (bet365 and kin). Odds shouting, casino urgency, green-on-green. Predictions must never read as tipster bait.
- **Generic AI SaaS**. Purple gradients, glowing orbs, "AI-powered" hero copy, identical card grids.
- **Legacy scores sites** (ESPN, Livescore). Ad-choked tables, banner clutter, dated chrome.
- **Fantasy hype content**. Clickbait framing, arrows, inflated numbers, YouTube-thumbnail energy.

## Design Principles

- **Fact and inference never blur.** Emerald is measured, amber is inferred. Every AI element carries confidence, generation date, and the sources it derived from. This is the product's integrity, not a styling choice.
- **Show your work.** Confidence beats certainty. A number the user can trace is worth more than a number that sounds impressive.
- **Speed is the aesthetic.** Keyboard-first, instant, no ceremony. Motion conveys state; it never performs. Chrome disappears into the task.
- **Depth without clutter.** Density is welcome — the audience wants it — but every element must earn its pixels. Reward attention, don't demand it.
- **Earn the return visit.** Personalization appears only once the user has given it something to work with. Empty until earned, never fake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Contrast validated on both dark and light surfaces, full keyboard operation (command palette, skip link, focus-visible everywhere), `prefers-reduced-motion` honored globally, and a chart palette validated for colorblind separation on both themes.
